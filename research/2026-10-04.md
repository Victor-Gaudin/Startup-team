Rules for this batch:

- Keep a candidate only if it has 10 or more sold listings in 30 days, and if
  `dropkit margin <cost> --shipping <ship>` gives a sale price at or below the
  typical sold price.
- On AliExpress, filter for "Ships from: United States". Buyers expect delivery
  within about 7 days, and Q4 shipping from China causes cancellations.
- Aim for a sale price of at least $18. Below about $10, eBay's fixed per-order
  fee eats the margin.
