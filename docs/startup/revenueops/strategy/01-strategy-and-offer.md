## 3. First offer (full structure)

**Name:** **TenderPulse** — *Weekly EU Public-Sector Opportunity Brief* (✅ approved)

**For:** Boutique digital / IT / marketing agencies & independent consultants in the EU (≈5–50 staff) that want public-sector contracts but have no dedicated bid/BD team to monitor portals daily.

**Problem:** Relevant public contracts are scattered across TED + dozens of national/regional portals. Agencies miss relevant tenders, find them too late to bid, or burn hours scanning.

**Urgency:** Tenders have hard deadlines — late means excluded. Pipeline must be continuous.

**Promise (truthful, no guarantees):** A curated weekly brief of currently-open opportunities matched to the client's services and regions — with deadlines, direct links, and a go/no-go suggestion — saving hours of scanning and reducing missed deadlines. *We do not promise contract wins.*

**You receive (deliverable):**
1. Weekly brief (PDF + Google Sheet): 10–25 matched opportunities. Each row: title, buyer, country/region, CPV category, published value (where available), submission deadline, direct TED/portal link, one-line relevance note, suggested go/no-go.
2. "What changed since last week" (new, closing-soon, awarded).
3. Monthly (Premium): category trend + recommended focus.

**Format:** PDF + Google Sheet (email delivery). **Frequency:** Weekly.

**Pricing ladder:**
- €49 — one-time sample brief (sales tool)
- €199/mo — Starter (1 service niche + region set)
- €499/mo — Premium (multi-region, deeper qualification, monthly trends)
- €999+/mo — Custom **[APPROVAL]**

**Proof:** The sourced sample at `sample-report/weekly-opportunity-brief-SAMPLE.md` (real data sources + exact structure).

**Fulfillment:** TED Open Data API + national portals + EU Funding & Tenders Portal → filter by CPV/keywords/region → AI-assisted curation → format & fact-check → deliver. Semi-automated.

**Data sources (all public/official):** [TED Open Data](https://data.ted.europa.eu/), [TED portal](https://ted.europa.eu/en/), national portals (e.g. BOAMP – France), [EU Funding & Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home).

**Compliance:** Public official B2B data only; use official open-data APIs; respect portal ToS/rate limits; GDPR-light (business-contact basis); maintain suppression list for outreach.

**Acquisition channels (priority):** inbound + niche communities → SEO landing page → low-volume compliant B2B outreach to agency BD leads **[APPROVAL]**.

**Sales message:** "Every week we send your agency the public-sector contracts you can actually bid on — matched to your services and regions, with deadlines and links — so you stop scanning portals and stop missing deadlines."

**Guarantee (safe):** "If we miss your scheduled delivery, that week is free." No win guarantees.

**Risks:** data freshness/accuracy; niche-match quality; established competitors (Hermix/BidStats); outreach compliance; owner-setup dependencies.

**Owner approvals before launch:** final brand name + sender identity; payment method; publish landing page; outreach (channel + template + volume + opt-out); any paid tool.
