## A. Landing page copy (EN)

**Headline:** The public-sector contracts your agency can actually bid on — every week.
**Subheadline:** TenderPulse monitors TED and national procurement portals and sends you a curated weekly brief of open tenders matched to your services and regions — with deadlines, links, and a go/no-go on each.

**Problem:** Billions in public contracts are posted across dozens of portals. Agencies miss the right ones, find them too late to bid, or lose hours scanning.

**What you get each week:**
- 10–25 opportunities matched to your service niche + regions
- Buyer, category (CPV), published value, deadline, and a direct link for each
- A one-line relevance note + suggested go / watch / skip
- "What changed since last week" — new, closing-soon, recent awards

**Pricing:** Starter €199/mo · Premium €499/mo (multi-region + monthly trends) · one-time sample €49.
**For:** EU digital/IT/marketing agencies & consultants (≈5–50 staff) targeting public-sector work without a dedicated bid team.
**Not for:** firms already running an in-house bid desk with monitoring software.
**Guarantee:** If we miss a scheduled delivery, that week is free. We do not guarantee contract wins.
**CTA:** Request your free sample brief.

## F. Reply-handling (pre-approved discovery question)

When a prospect replies positively, ask one clarifying question, then deliver a tailored sample:
> EN: "Great — to make the sample genuinely useful: which 1–2 service lines and which countries/regions should I match opportunities to?"
> FR: "Parfait — pour un exemple vraiment utile : quels 1–2 services et quels pays/régions dois-je cibler ?"
> ES: "Genial — para que el ejemplo sea útil: ¿qué 1–2 servicios y qué países/regiones debo priorizar?"

Anything involving custom scope, custom pricing, or commitments → draft a proposal for owner approval first (see `strategy/01` proposal structure).

## G. Compliance rules baked into outreach
- Max 10–20 personalised messages/day. One contact per company until they reply.
- Personalise the first line with a real, specific observation about the agency.
- Always include the opt-out line. Honour opt-outs **immediately** → add to `crm/suppression-list.csv`.
- No attachments unless requested. No deceptive subject lines. No false urgency.
- Log every send in `crm/outreach-log.csv`. Stop and alert owner if complaints appear.
- Business contacts only; minimise personal data (GDPR / ePrivacy legitimate-interest basis for B2B).
