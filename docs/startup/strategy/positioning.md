# Positioning Framework: AI Agent Payments Dashboard

## One-Liner
The Stripe dashboard for AI agents.

## Tagline
See what your agents spend.

## 3-Sentence Pitch
AI agents are increasingly acting as economic agents, but developers lack the visibility and control needed to manage their transactions safely. Our platform provides a dedicated UX layer on top of Stripe, offering AI agents their own payment accounts with enforceable spend limits and real-time transaction monitoring. We enable developers to give their agents financial autonomy without the risk of runaway costs or opaque billing.

## Positioning Statement vs. Competitors
Unlike general cloud hosting (Vercel, Fly.io) that focuses on compute, or raw payment APIs (Stripe) that require complex custom wrappers, **AI Agent Payments Dashboard** is a dedicated financial OS for the agentic era. We prioritize **Safety** (spend limits), **Visibility** (transaction dashboards), and **Autonomy** (programmatic authorization), making it the first choice for developers who want their agents to pay their own way.

## Target Personas
1. **The Solo Builder:** Launching an autonomous agent that needs to pay for its own API credits and tools.
2. **The Agentic Startup:** A small team building a fleet of agents that buy/sell on behalf of users.
3. **The AI Freelancer:** A developer creating "Agents as a Service" that earn revenue and pay their own operating costs.
